Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

Times ("the App", "we", "us") was built on a foundational principle: your schedule is personal, and your time data belongs entirely to you. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices.

01Information We Collect

We collect zero personal information. Times does not collect, record, process, or transmit any data about you or your device. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, or phone number is requested or collected.
  • No user account, profile, or login system exists.
  • No analytics SDKs, telemetry, or behavioral tracking tools are integrated.
  • No crash-reporting services that send stack traces off-device are used.
  • No advertising networks or marketing trackers are included.
  • No location data, contact lists, or advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) are accessed.

02On-Device Storage

Everything you create in Times — daily schedules, time blocks, custom activities, tags, weekly presets, and application settings — is stored exclusively on your device inside the application's sandboxed local container using SQLite (via Drift). No network requests are made during normal use.

03Backup, Export & Import

Times includes a local database export feature allowing you to preserve or transfer your data. When you tap "Export database", a SQLite database file (.db) is generated in the device's temporary cache and passed to the native system share sheet. You choose where to save or send that file (such as your local Files app or AirDrop). Times never transmits this backup to any external server.

Similarly, when importing a database, the file is read directly from the location you select and copied into local application storage.

04Local Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, the App schedules a local alert through your device's operating system. These alerts run completely offline and require no remote push notification server.

05Device Permissions

Times requests only the minimum system permissions necessary to function:

Platform Permission Purpose
iOS UserNotifications Show optional daily reminder notification to plan your day.
Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show optional daily reminder notification.
Android RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restore local daily reminder schedule after device reboot.

06Third-Party Services & Bundled Assets

Times bundles all required assets (including font files) directly inside the app binary. The App makes no external network calls at runtime to load fonts, styles, or scripts.

When downloading or updating Times, you interact with Apple App Store or Google Play Store under their respective privacy policies.

07Children's Privacy

Because Times does not collect or transmit any data whatsoever, the App is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and applicable global child safety frameworks. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 or of any age.

08Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because all data remains exclusively on your device, you have immediate and permanent control over your data:

  • You can clear individual days or delete blocks at any time directly in the planner.
  • Uninstalling the App from your device permanently purges all local data and database files.
  • Because we store no copies of your data on servers, there is no remote data for us to retain, disclose, or delete.
