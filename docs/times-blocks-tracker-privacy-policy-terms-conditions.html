Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Daily Blocks: Schedule Planner ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal time blocking planner. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. The app is designed so that your schedule stays on your device.

01Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from you. The app does not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive your time blocks or planner entries.

Your planner content — blocks, activities, tags, presets, and dates — is stored locally in the app's sandboxed storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. The app does not collect data, does not track you, and does not share data with data brokers or advertisers.

02Accounts and tracking

No account is required. The app does not include third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or rent personal information.

03Exports and sharing

You can export your database as a file. When you export or share, the file leaves the app only because you chose an action in the system share dialog. The destination app or service you choose handles that file under its own privacy policy.

04Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent through a server. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your device settings.

05Fonts and assets

The app bundles its fonts locally. The app does not contact Google Fonts or any external font service at runtime — no network font request is made by the app. This website uses Google Fonts to display its typography; that is a separate request made by your browser when you visit this page and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

06Third-party services

  • Your device's system share dialogs and file picker, used only when you export or import a backup.
  • The app marketplace you use, such as the App Store, for app distribution and updates.
  • Your device's system browser, used only when you tap a Privacy Policy or Terms link.

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not share your schedule or planner data with these services.

07Data retention and deletion

Your entries remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual blocks or clear days in the app. Removing the app deletes its local data, so export a backup first if you want to keep your history. Because we do not receive your data, we have no copy to delete on our side.

08Your rights

You control your data directly in the app. Because the app stores your planner locally and we do not receive it, you can exercise your rights using the app's own tools (adding, editing, exporting, and deleting blocks) and by uninstalling the app.

09Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10Security

Your schedule is stored in the app's sandboxed storage on your device. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode and keeping your operating system up to date. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will always be available on this page. If a change is material, we will update the date above.
