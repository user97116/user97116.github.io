Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Moods Tracker ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal mood journal. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. The app is designed so that your journal stays on your device.

01Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from you. The app does not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive your journal entries.

Content you create, such as moods, activities, notes, dates, and settings, is stored locally in the app's storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us.

02Accounts and tracking

No account is required. The app does not include third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or rent personal information.

03Exports and sharing

You can export your data as a file. When you export or share, the file leaves the app only because you chose an action in the system save or share dialog. The app or service you send it to handles that file under its own privacy policy.

04Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent through a server. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your device settings.

05Fonts

The app may download typeface assets from Google Fonts so it can display its chosen font. No mood entries or notes are included in those requests. When fonts are downloaded, Google may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and device or browser details as part of serving the request, under Google's privacy policy.

06Third-party services

  • Google Fonts, for typeface delivery.
  • Your device's system share and save dialogs, for exports.
  • Apple App Store or Google Play, for app distribution and updates.

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not share your journal entries or notes with these services. Any third party that processes data in connection with the app is required to provide protections consistent with this policy and applicable law.

07Data retention and deletion

Your entries remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual entries in the app. Removing the app deletes its local data, so export a backup first if you want to keep your history. Because we do not receive your data, we have no copy to delete on our side.

08Your rights

You control your data directly in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information. Because the app stores your journal locally and we do not receive it, you can exercise these rights using the app's own tools (viewing, editing, exporting, and deleting entries) and by uninstalling the app. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your entries or uninstalling the app.

09Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, remember that any content they created stays on the device and can be removed by deleting entries or uninstalling the app.

10Security

Your journal is stored in the app's sandboxed storage on your device. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode and keeping your operating system up to date. No method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will always be available in Settings and on this page. If a change is material, we will update the date above.
