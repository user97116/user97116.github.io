Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-10-17  |  Last Updated: 2026-10-17

Summary

Habits is a fully offline, local-only application. The Application does not require an account and does not send your personal data to the Service Provider's servers.

Data you enter into the Application is stored locally on your device. The Application does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with the Service Provider or third parties.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Application does not intentionally collect, transmit, or share personal data. Specifically:

  • No user account or registration is required.
  • No data is sent to the Service Provider's servers or cloud services.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs are used.
  • No advertising identifiers or device identifiers are intentionally accessed for tracking.
  • The Application does not collect your IP address.
  • Your habit data, including habits, routines, categories, completion history, streaks, subtasks, and progress values, remains on your device and is not transmitted by the Application.
  • The Application does not use cookies, pixels, or web tracking technologies.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Data that you create in the Application, including habits, descriptions, categories, icons and colors, completion history, streaks, subtasks, measurable progress values, reminder times, and app preferences, is stored locally on your device.

This data is:

  • Stored within the Application's local storage.
  • Not transmitted by the Application to the Service Provider.
  • Not uploaded by the Application to a cloud service.
  • Not shared by the Service Provider with third parties.
  • Generally removed when the Application is uninstalled, subject to your device's operating-system behavior and any copies you may have exported.

The Service Provider does not have access to data stored only within the Application on your device.

3. Backup and Data Export

The Application provides an optional manual backup/export feature that allows you to save a copy of your local database to a location you choose using your device's system save dialog.

You may also choose to share the exported backup using your device's share sheet.

  • Exporting a backup is entirely user-initiated.
  • The Application does not automatically export or upload your backup.
  • You are responsible for choosing where the exported file is stored or shared.
  • Exported backup files may contain sensitive personal information about your habits and routines.
  • Once a backup is saved outside the Application's private storage or shared with another application or service, the Service Provider does not control or have access to that copy.
  • The privacy and security of an exported file may therefore depend on the destination you choose.

4. Android Backup

The Application's Android Auto Backup functionality is disabled.

The Application does not intentionally synchronize its local habit data to Google Drive or another cloud service through Android Auto Backup.

5. Notifications, Reminders, and Widgets

The Application may offer optional local reminders and home-screen widgets. These features are entirely local:

  • Notifications and reminders are scheduled on your device and do not use any push or cloud service.
  • If you enable timely reminders, the Application may ask your device for the exact-alarm permission; if it is not granted, reminders fall back to less precise timing, and you can change this at any time in your device settings.
  • Home-screen widgets display habit information that is stored locally in the Application's private storage; no widget data leaves your device.
  • Notification content and widget contents are not transmitted anywhere by the Application.

6. Third-Party Libraries

The Application uses open-source libraries to provide functionality such as local database storage, preferences storage, file selection, sharing, charts, state management, date formatting, local notifications, and the home-screen widget bridge.

These libraries are used for the Application's local functionality. The Application does not intentionally use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or data-collection services.

The share_plus functionality is used to open the device's native sharing interface when you explicitly choose to share a file.

The file_picker functionality is used to open system file-selection or save dialogs when you explicitly use those features.

7. Children

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the Application does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information, the Service Provider does not maintain a database of children's personal information.

Parents or guardians should supervise a child's use of applications according to the requirements applicable in their jurisdiction.

8. Your Privacy Rights

The Service Provider does not maintain your habit data on its servers.

Because your Application data remains on your device, you can manage or delete it directly within the Application or by uninstalling the Application, subject to any exported copies you have created.

You can:

  • Delete individual habits, entries, and records within the Application.
  • Use the Application's available clear/delete functions.
  • Uninstall the Application.
  • Delete exported backup files from the locations where you saved them.

We recommend that you:

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The Service Provider does not control the security of your device or any third-party destination where you voluntarily export or share your data.
